4. POLICY DESK (http://localhost:5173/policies)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Admins and Agents use this to issue new insurance coverage:

* ISSUE NEW INSURANCE POLICY FORM FIELDS
  - Customer ID:            1 (Must refer to an existing Customer ID, e.g., 1 or 2)
  - Policy Plan/Type:       [Select plan from dropdown list]
                            - Life Secure Term Plan (Coverage: $1,000,000, Premium Rate: 0.5%)
                            - Health Guard Premium (Coverage: $500,000, Premium Rate: 2.0%)
                            - Motor Vehicle Policy (Coverage: $300,000, Premium Rate: 1.5%)
                            - Property Protection Plan (Coverage: $2,500,000, Premium Rate: 0.3%)
  - Sum Assured:            150000 (Maximum coverage depends on the plan chosen above)

  *Note: The backend automatically computes the yearly Premium.
   Example: Sum Assured = $150,000 for Health Guard (2%) -> Premium = $3,000.00.

5. PREMIUM PAYMENTS & BILLING (http://localhost:5173/payments)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Used to activate "Pending" policies or pay renewal fees:

* PAY INSURANCE INVOICE FORM FIELDS
  - Policy ID:              1 (The ID of the policy to pay, e.g. 1, 2, or 3)
  - Premium Amount ($):     3000.00 (Must match the calculated premium of that policy)
  - Payment Channel:        [Select from dropdown]
                            - Credit Card
                            - Debit Card
                            - UPI Payment
                            - Net Banking
                            - Cash Ledger
  *Result: On click, policy status changes from "Pending" to "Active", and the receipt becomes downloadable.

6. CLAIMS CLEARANCE OFFICE (http://localhost:5173/claims)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Filed by Customers when requesting reimbursement:

* FILE INSURANCE CLAIM FORM FIELDS
  - Policy ID:                  1 (The ID of the active policy to claim against)
  - Customer ID:                1 (Associated Customer ID)
  - Date of Incident:           2026-07-10 (YYYY-MM-DD format)
  - Claim Settlement Amount ($): 1500.00 (Must not exceed the policy's sum assured)
  - Incident Description:       "Minor collision at a traffic intersection resulting in front bumper damage. Replaced the bumper at authorized workshop. Uploading invoice details."

  *Clearance Action by Admin/Agent:
   - Click "Review" to change status to "Under Review".
   - Click "Approve" or "Reject".
   - Click "Settle Funds" (for approved claims) to disburse the claims payout.
